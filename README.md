# Arnik

Official website for **Arnik**, a creative and digital studio focused on helping businesses build stronger digital identities and online experiences.

Arnik is built to showcase our services, selected work, capabilities, and team while providing a clear way for potential clients to explore what we do and get in touch with us.

## About Arnik

Arnik is a small creative team working across digital products, web experiences, branding, and content.

The website serves as the primary online presence for the studio and is designed to communicate our capabilities, showcase our work, and help potential clients understand how we can work together.

## Project Goals

* Establish a professional digital presence for Arnik
* Present the studio's services and capabilities
* Showcase selected projects and previous work
* Introduce the team behind the studio
* Create a clear path for potential clients to get in touch
* Deliver a responsive and modern experience across different devices

## Tech Stack

* **React**
* Modern JavaScript ecosystem

The dependency stack is currently being reviewed and simplified to remove unnecessary packages and keep the project lightweight and maintainable.

## Development

The initial implementation of the website was created with extensive use of **AI-assisted development tools** based on the project's design direction and business requirements.

AI was used to accelerate the implementation process, while the overall product direction, content, design decisions, refinement, integration, and final technical decisions remain part of the project's development workflow.
